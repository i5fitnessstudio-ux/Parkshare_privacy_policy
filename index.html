ParkShare Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information ParkShare collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it and the choices you have. It applies to both parking owners and vehicle users of the ParkShare app.

1. Information We Collect

Account information: your full name, mobile number, email address, password (stored securely hashed, never in plain text) and, for parking owners, your UPI ID.

Profile and listing information: the parking details you submit, including address, coordinates, plot and gate dimensions, space counts, amenities, access hours, rent and the photographs you upload.

Booking and vehicle information: the space you book, the booking reference, the period of the booking, the vehicle registration number, make and model, and the payment method and transaction reference used.

Technical information: your device model, operating system, app version, IP address, crash and diagnostic logs, and the pages or screens you use.

Communications: messages you send to another user through the in-app chat, and any correspondence you have with our support team.

2. How We Use Your Information

To create and operate your account, to show you your bookings, listings, earnings, chat history and notifications.

To match vehicle users with parking spaces: your approximate or exact location is used to search nearby listings and to show the parking spaces on the map.

To process payments, issue receipts, record Cash on Delivery collections and calculate owner earnings.

To prevent fraud and abuse, including duplicate accounts, fake listings, no-shows and attempts to circumvent slot limits.

To communicate with you about a booking, a listing, a payment or a change to the platform.

To understand how the app is used so we can fix errors and improve it. We rely on aggregated and de-identified usage data for this and do not use it to identify you individually.

To meet legal, tax and regulatory obligations.

3. How We Share Your Information

With the other party to a booking: an owner sees the name, contact number, vehicle details and booking period of a user who books their space, and a user sees the owner's name, contact number and UPI ID for that booking. This information is shared only because the booking cannot be completed without it.

With our payment partners, to the extent needed to process a UPI or to record a Cash on Delivery collection and issue a receipt.

With service providers who host our servers, send messages, deliver push notifications or provide analytics, under contract and only for the purpose we specify.

With authorities or litigants where disclosure is required by law, by a court order, or where necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of ParkShare, our users or the public.

With a buyer or successor in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of all or part of ParkShare, in which case your information will continue to be handled under this policy.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties for their own marketing.

4. Payments and Financial Information

Payment processing is handled by third-party payment service providers. We never see or store your full card number, PIN, CVV or UPI PIN.

We keep only what we need to administer a booking: the payment method, the amount, the transaction or receipt reference, the status and the timestamp of the payment or cash collection.

Parking owners see the amount and the payment reference for their own earnings. Vehicle users see the amount they paid and the refund or receipt details for their own bookings.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

The app stores your session token on the device so you stay signed in, and small amounts of preference data such as your chosen role and map settings.

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party advertising trackers.

Signing out removes the session data from the device. If you uninstall the app, local data is deleted with it.

6. Location Data and Permissions

The app asks for location permission to show your position on the map, to search for parking near you, to drop a pin for a listing, and to record the location of a booking.

If you decline the permission, you can still use the app by searching for an area or an address instead of using your current position.

You can turn location access off at any time in your device settings. Without it, some map features will not work.

Approximate location is used for the general map search; the precise coordinates you choose for a listing are stored with that listing and shown to users who book it.

7. Data Retention

We keep account and booking information for as long as your account is active, because it is needed to administer your bookings, listings and earnings.

Financial and transaction records are retained for the period required by applicable law, including tax and accounting obligations.

Messages you delete in the app may still be retained where we must keep them to resolve a dispute or comply with a legal obligation.

When you close your account we delete or anonymise your personal information, except for the records we are legally required to retain.

8. Data Security

Your password is stored as a salted hash and is never stored or transmitted in plain text. All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit.

Access to personal information is restricted to staff and service providers who need it to operate the platform, and is granted on a least-privilege basis.

No system is completely secure. If we become aware of a breach that affects your information, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

9. Your Rights and Choices

You can view and edit your profile, listings, bookings and transactions from within the app.

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account. Write to us at the address in the contact section and we will respond within the period required by law.

Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Consent to these Terms and to this Privacy Policy is collected on the Create Account screen and is required to use the app.

You may choose not to grant location, camera or photo-library permission, and you may opt out of promotional messages by using the unsubscribe link in any message.

We do not sell your personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of on that front.

10. Children's Privacy

ParkShare is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

If you believe a child has created an account or provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The version in force is always the one published in the app, with its last updated date shown on this screen.

Where a change materially affects how we use information you have already given us, we will tell you before the change takes effect.

Your continued use of the app after a change is published means you accept the updated Privacy Policy.
